## DEC-12 provisional owner recommendation (16 Nov 2026)

**This is a proposed Phase 1 pilot operating policy supplied by the project owner. It is not final client-approved business policy and must not be described as such. It is subject to client confirmation and independent security review.** Final client policy for this decision: **not yet confirmed** (approver role and date to be recorded here).

- Field officers may create farmer/project enrollments only when explicitly granted `enrollment.create`, constrained by active organization, project and geographical assignments; current `farmer.read` and the current project-enrollment policy must also authorize the operation. Creating or registering a farmer never grants enrollment authority.
- Authorized field officers may queue offline enrollment intents on approved organization devices using the existing encrypted vault. The server revalidates permissions, farmer status, current geography and project policy at synchronization.
- Officers cannot withdraw enrollments in v1; withdrawal needs a separately authorized administrator with `enrollment.withdraw`. No additional per-enrollment manual approval in v1; eligibility and authorization remain mandatory. Enrollment permissions are never seeded to all field officers; grants must be explicit, scoped and audited.
- **Offline farmer dependency:** only server-confirmed farmers may be selected for offline enrollment; dependent mutation ordering is deferred to a separate future ADR.
- **Refusal reasons:** field officers see generic messages; authorized administrators may see detailed reasons and audit history through properly scoped server endpoints (new work, ENR-AUDIT-01). Local `rejected`, `conflict` and `unconfirmed` states stay distinct.
- **Offline queue limits (technical safeguards, not client-approved expiry rules, configurable):** at most 100 unresolved intents per account/device vault; capacity and 7-day warnings; escalation after 30 days; never auto-delete an unsynchronized intent; bounded retry with stable mutation IDs; age never bypasses current server authorization.

Approval steps before ADR 0021 may be marked Accepted, and the later enablement gate, are listed in the ADR (sections 9 and 10).

## Outside current Phase 1

Farmer self-service is Phase 2. Harvest procurement/logistics/transport, advanced financial settlement, insurance and legal land-survey workflows remain excluded. Questions about those future responsibilities are not blockers that require implementing those modules in this pilot; an explicit scope change is required first.
