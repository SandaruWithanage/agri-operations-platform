## Design questions that P1-13 (overlap) must settle before code

1. **Detail within scope.** Overlap detail (candidate overlap area and the other parcel's identity) is computed only against polygons the reviewer can already see through row-level security. This is the direct reading of FR-LAND-015 and the architecture's "no unauthorized neighbor identity".
2. **Existence across scope.** Quota must not double-count an overlap with a polygon the officer cannot see. A bare "this parcel overlaps something outside your scope" flag avoids identity but still reveals that a polygon exists at that place. The owner must decide whether that minimal disclosure is acceptable, or whether the flag is visible only to a broader reviewer and the officer sees only "needs review".
3. **Who resolves.** Reviewer separation (the person who captured a polygon must not clear its overlap), an effective-dated resolution history, and legitimate shared parcels (several cultivators on one plot) need the DEC-02 role matrix and DEC-09 overlap tolerance; neither exists yet.
4. **No automatic rejection.** An overlapping polygon is saved provisionally; only the conflicted portion is excluded from verified or allocatable area until resolved. Mixed `Polygon`/`MultiPolygon`/`GeometryCollection` intersection results, slivers and tolerance need reviewed functions and fixtures (architecture 6.3 caution).
5. **Aggregates.** Counts and area totals must not let a regional viewer infer another region's totals through cross-filtering (FR-LAND-019).
