## Decision hierarchy and non-negotiable gates

1. This register is a **provisional owner decision register, not a signed client SRS, approved change order, final legal policy, security certification or authorization to deploy**.
2. Existing **accepted technical architecture remains in effect** unless changed by a separately reviewed ADR. A new owner proposal is a change candidate, not an implicit approval to bypass a security/implementation gate.
3. **ADR 0021 remains NOT Accepted**. The new same-session farmer→enrollment dependency (Q3) requires a design amendment and independent review before implementing OFF-04A–D; no real-officer enablement until client/security/real-device gates and a separate owner enablement decision.
4. Q48 **final answer B** overrides the earlier provisional Q48 C. Walking perimeter capture in Q52 is an additional design condition, not a replacement for manual editing and survey import.
5. Financial settlement, procurement, transport, farmer self-service and legal title surveying are _not_ automatically authorized by the GIS/inventory/reporting choices. Any expanded feature requires explicit scope approval.
6. The Q71 Sinhala client explanation must match the English SRS/quotation numbers, and give clear module-by-module what, why, how, duration, cost and cost rationale. Estimates and dates are **not** established by the 71 decisions.

## Dependencies and unresolved approvals after the 71 questions

- **DEC-01:** legal managing organization, authorized client approver(s), product name/branding, actual organizational and geographic configuration still need client confirmation.
- **DEC-10 source arithmetic:** a 2026/27 agricultural plan's four category rows total **2,760**, while the printed total says **2,700**. Q56 creates a reconciliation process; it does **not** decide which figure is correct. Must not silently import/promote either.
- **DEC-02:** exact person-by-person permission and project/geographic assignment matrix, high-risk classifications and approver identities still require configuration and client approval.
- **DEC-07:** actual supported device models, mobile browser permissions, physical security posture, seven-day local expiry handling, lost-key and raw-GPS background behavior require tests; password-recovery design is not an encryption bypass.
- **DEC-08:** provider/region/domain, monthly budget, key custody, backup retention and RPO/RTO restore feasibility are not settled; VPS cron-based secret rotation needs a reviewed safe plan.
- **DEC-09:** approved tile/imagery license, authoritative boundary datasets, geometry and GPS accuracy thresholds, permissible raw-track retention, land survey verification authority must be approved; a phone-recorded polygon is not legal title or a cadastral survey.
- **DEC-11:** UAT approvers, native-language reviewers, real field-device proof, independent security sign-off, acceptance cases, training quantities and paid support terms remain open.
- **Scope and commercial baseline:** comprehensive reporting and full GIS from day one imply a re-estimate. The previously discussed LKR 200,000–400,000 is **not an accepted quote for the expanded scope**. Q71 prohibits unapproved silent cuts.
