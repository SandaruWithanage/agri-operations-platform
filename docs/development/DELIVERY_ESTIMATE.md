# Phase 1 engineering delivery estimate

Assessment date: 16 November 2026, against merged main through PR #29 (`8ef96ac`) plus the locally tested configurable project catalog in this change. **Approximately 55% complete / 45% remaining**, with a rough 50–60% completion range. This is an engineering judgment against the current [Phase 1 scope and backlog](PHASE_1_STATUS.md), not a measured burn-up, fixed delivery date, client acceptance or production-readiness score. The initial M0 foundation itself is complete.

The estimate weights unfinished operational modules and production work rather than counting files, tests or merged PRs. Passing automated tests demonstrates specific behaviors; a test count does not measure completed requirements. Later commits/tests do not automatically change the percentage. Reassess after substantive workflow delivery and confirmed client scope. Farmer self-service (Phase 2), financial/procurement/insurance and advanced legal surveying are excluded from this Phase 1 estimate.
